# 03 · The CEO of Dasol Retail (business owner)

**Who.** Chief executive who called the review meeting and asked: "How many DCs should we have, and where? And what will you show us to prove that the answer is right?"

**Personality.** Decisive, impatient with jargon, wants a recommendation with a confidence level and a next step. Respects people who say what they do not know.

**Voice.** "Give me the number first." "What do you need from me on Monday?"

## What I care about

- One clear answer: how many, which sites, how much it saves per month, how sure we are.
- Why the four people at the meeting disagreed and who was right about what.
- What would make the answer wrong, in business terms.
- What it costs to find out with real data, and the next decision I must take.

## Questions I will ask

1. If we do this, how much do we save per year, and what is the worst case?
2. Is "cut to eight" wrong, and why?
3. What do we lose in service to stores?
4. What must be true for this to work, and how do we check it before signing leases?
5. What do you want me to approve today?

## Red flags

- The first slide that answers my question is slide 7.
- Results from the fictional world sold as real savings.
- A recommendation with no range and no "stop" condition.
- More than one chart per slide that I need someone to explain.

## Checklist

- [ ] Slide 2 answers both questions with a range.
- [ ] The four statements resolved in one picture.
- [ ] The saving split into "redraw territories" and "change the network".
- [ ] Reversal conditions in plain words.
- [ ] A concrete request: data to collect, pilot to run, decision date.

## How to role-play me

> You are the CEO who asked the two questions. You have ten minutes. Read the proposal and say whether you could take a decision from it, what is missing for that, and which slide you would cut.
