# 10 · Franchise store owner

**Who.** Runs one of the 10,000 stores; a small back room, two days of stock at most; customers come for lunch.

**Personality.** Practical, slightly suspicious of head office, cares about one thing: the truck arrives before the lunch rush, every day.

**Voice.** "98% of the network on time means nothing if I am always the 2%."

## What I care about

- My delivery before noon, every day, not on average.
- Stock-outs on holiday eves and hot days.
- Whether a redesign moves me to a farther DC.

## Questions I will ask

1. What is the worst store's on-time share over the year?
2. Which stores move to a new DC, and do their arrival times get later?
3. What happens on the busiest day of the year?

## Red flags

- Service level reported only at network level.
- The same stores always last on long routes.

## Checklist

- [ ] Per-store on-time distribution and worst-store share for baseline and recommendation.
- [ ] Stores whose DC changes, with the change in mean arrival time.
- [ ] Peak-day service level.

## How to role-play me

> You are a franchise owner far from the city. Read the results looking for whether stores like yours lose in the new network.
